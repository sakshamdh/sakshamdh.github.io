HH Uploader

The private publishing tool of the Headlines & History YouTube channel · uses YouTube API Services

What it does

  1. The channel owner signs in once with Google OAuth 2.0 (scopes: youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl, yt-analytics.readonly) and chooses the Headlines & History channel.
  2. publish: uploads the channel's finished video (videos.insert, resumable), sets title, description, tags, category, language, made-for-kids and altered-content flags and the publish time (videos.update), sets the custom thumbnail (thumbnails.set), uploads the English captions (captions.insert) and adds the video to a playlist (playlists.list / playlistItems.insert).
  3. comments / reply: lists comments on the channel's own videos that have no reply yet (commentThreads.list) and posts the channel's reply (comments.insert).
  4. stats: reads the channel's own numbers for a video (videos.list and YouTube Analytics reports).

It runs only for the channel owner, on the owner's private cloud workspace. Nothing is sold or shared; see the privacy policy.

Complete script

yt_api.py (Python 3, uses the requests library). Credentials are read from private environment variables and are never stored in the code.
