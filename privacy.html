Privacy Policy

HH Uploader (Headlines & History) · last updated 18 November 2026

What this tool is

HH Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Headlines & History YouTube channel to manage that channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

  • The channel owner's own YouTube channel: its videos, playlists, captions and thumbnails (to upload and update them).
  • Public comments posted on the channel's own videos (to read them and reply as the channel).
  • The channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time and similar figures).

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish and manage the Headlines & History channel. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or combined with other data. The access token is stored privately in the owner's own secure environment and is never published. Comment text is processed only to write replies and is not kept after that.

Cookies

This tool does not place cookies on any visitor's device. This website does not use cookies or tracking.

Removing access

The channel owner can remove this tool's access at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Data obtained through YouTube API Services is deleted within 30 days after access is removed or on request.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact details on the Headlines & History channel page.
